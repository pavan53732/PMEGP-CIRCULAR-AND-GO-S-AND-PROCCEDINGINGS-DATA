# PMEGP Archive Collection Policy

This document outlines the collection guidelines, quality standards, and compliance policies for the **Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) Circular, GO & Proceedings Data Archive**.

---

## 1. Scope of Collection

The archive is strictly limited to official, authenticated documents that directly or indirectly govern, implement, allocate, clarify, or report on the **Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)** in India, with a primary focus on the State of **Andhra Pradesh**.

### Included Documents:
- **Central Government Policies:** Guidelines, circulars, notifications, and letters issued by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME), Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC), and Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **AP State Government Policies:** Government Orders (GOs), circulars, and memorandums issued by the Industries & Commerce Dept, Finance Dept, or Rural Development Dept.
- **Administrative Directives:** Proceedings, instructions, guidelines, and circulars issued by the Commissioner of Industries, AP, and the KVIC State Office in Vijayawada/Visakhapatnam.
- **District-Level Records:** Proceedings from District Collectors, District Industries Centres (DICs), District Level Consultative Committees (DLCCs), District Level Review Committees (DLRCs), and Lead Bank Offices (LBOs).
- **Banking Guidelines:** Agenda, minutes, and reports from the State Level Bankers' Committee (SLBC) and individual commercial banks.

---

## 2. Permitted Official Sources

To maintain academic and administrative integrity, documents must only be sourced from **official, authorized portals** or validated official channels.

### Primary Sourcing Directory:
1. **Andhra Pradesh Government Orders:** [ap.gov.in](https://ap.gov.in), [ap.gov.in/gazette](https://ap.gov.in/gazette)
2. **AP Industries Department:** [industries.ap.gov.in](https://industries.ap.gov.in)
3. **Ministry of MSME:** [msme.gov.in](https://msme.gov.in), [pmegp.msme.gov.in](https://pmegp.msme.gov.in)
4. **KVIC Central Office:** [kvic.gov.in](https://kvic.gov.in)
5. **SLBC Andhra Pradesh:** [slbcap.nic.in](https://slbcap.nic.in)
6. **District Portals:** Official district portals (e.g., `https://anakapalli.ap.gov.in`, `https://visakhapatnam.ap.gov.in`)

### Secondary Sourcing (RTI & Physical Collection):
- **Right to Information (RTI) Requests:** Documents obtained through official RTI filings to State Public Information Officers (SPIOs).
- **Physical Archives:** Certified photocopies or scans of official circulars from DIC/KVIC/Lead Bank offices.
- *Any secondary source must be clearly declared in the metadata notes field.*

---

## 3. Privacy & Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

Some administrative proceedings (such as subsidy sanction lists or district DLCC approval lists) contain personal information of beneficiaries. To comply with privacy standards, **strict redaction protocols must be followed**.

### Redaction Protocol:
Before committing any PDF/image to the repository, you **MUST** black out or strip:
- **Aadhaar Numbers** (12-digit unique IDs)
- **Bank Account Numbers** and IFSC codes
- **Personal Contact Details** (Phone numbers, email addresses, exact home addresses)
- **Detailed Personal Financial Data** (except aggregated subsidy/sanction amounts)

*Any document found to contain unredacted PII will be immediately removed, redacted, and recommitted.*

---

## 4. Document Verification Standards

Every uploaded document is classified by its verification status:
1. **Verified:** Cross-referenced against an active, live official URL or certified physical stamp.
2. **Unverified:** Transferred from another private researcher or obtained via a secondary channel without verifiable digital signatures or stamps.
3. **Disputed:** Flagged by contributors or maintainers due to formatting, content discrepancies, or suspected tampering.

---

## 5. Licensing and Copyright

- **Government Documents:** As per Section 52(1)(q) of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, the reproduction of any Act, Government Order, circular, or proceeding is generally permitted. However, documents remain under the administrative ownership of their respective issuing bodies.
