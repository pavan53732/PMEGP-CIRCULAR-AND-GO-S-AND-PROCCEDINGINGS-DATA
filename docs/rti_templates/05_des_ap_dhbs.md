# RTI Application — Priority 5: Directorate of Economics and Statistics, AP

**Target PIO**: Public Information Officer, Directorate of Economics and Statistics, Government of Andhra Pradesh.

**Priority**: 5 — DES District Handbooks (DHB) for missing districts and years.

**Estimated Fee**: ₹50 (general) / ₹10 (BPL with certificate)

**Expected Response Time**: 30 days from filing

**Background**: The DES AP portal (`des.ap.gov.in`) publishes District Handbooks (DHB) annually. The 2013-14 editions for Vizianagaram, Prakasam, and Krishna were acquired via the Wayback Machine (see `AP_VZM-DIC-2018-0001`, `AP_PKM-DIC-2018-0001`, `AP_KRI-DIC-2018-0001`). However, the Visakhapatnam DHB was not archived, and no DHBs appear to exist for years after 2013-14 on the public portal. The DHBs contain Chapter 9 "Industries" with data sourced directly from the General Manager of the District Industries Centre (DIC) — making them the only online-accessible proxy for direct DIC proceedings.

## Why This RTI Matters

The DES District Handbooks are the **only online-accessible source of district-level DIC data**. While they don't contain PMEGP-specific keywords (the DHB series predates the explicit PMEGP data tables in the Socio-Economic Survey), Chapter 9 "Industries" of each DHB contains:

- **Small Scale Industries** (mandal-wise) — sourced from GM, DIC
- **Cottage Industries** — sourced from GM, DIC
- **Working Factories** (registered under Factories Act, 1948)
- **Large and Medium Scale Industries**
- **Employment Exchange Registrations & Placements**

This data provides the **industrial baseline** against which PMEGP-assisted micro enterprises can be compared. For Visakhapatnam and Anakapalli specifically, the DHBs would give us:

- The total number of small scale industries in each mandal (context for PMEGP unit density)
- The cottage industry distribution (which PMEGP-eligible activities are most prevalent)
- Employment exchange data (which indicates the unemployed youth population that PMEGP targets)

Combined with the FY 2024-25 PMEGP progress data in [`docs/DISTRICT_PMEGP_PROGRESS_FY2024_25.md`](../DISTRICT_PMEGP_PROGRESS_FY2024_25.md), the DHBs would allow researchers to calculate PMEGP penetration rates (PMEGP units per 1000 small scale industries) per district.
