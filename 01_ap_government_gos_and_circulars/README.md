# 01 - AP Government GOs & Circulars

## What goes here

Government Orders (GOs) and Circulars issued by the **Government of Andhra Pradesh** that relate to PMEGP implementation, subsidy disbursement, or micro/small enterprise promotion.

## Typical issuing departments

- **Department of Industries, Commerce & Exports** — primary PMEGP-related GOs
- **Department of Finance** — budget allocations, subsidy release orders
- **Department of Rural Development** — PMEGP components under rural employment schemes
- **Chief Minister's Office** — high-level policy announcements

## Document identification

AP Government Orders follow the pattern: `GO.Ms.No. XXX / Department / Date`
Example: `GO.Ms.No. 42 / Industries & Commerce (Ind) Dept. / dated: 15-03-2024`

## Naming convention

```
PMEGP-AP-YYYY-NNN_descriptive_slug.pdf
PMEGP-AP-YYYY-NNN_descriptive_slug.metadata.json
```

## Notes

- Many older GOs (pre-2014, undivided AP) may reference "Combined Andhra Pradesh" and may still be relevant
- Some GOs may be in Telugu only — flag these with `"language": "te"` in metadata
- If a GO is referenced in a proceeding but the original GO file is unavailable, create a placeholder metadata with `"source_url": null` and note the reference
