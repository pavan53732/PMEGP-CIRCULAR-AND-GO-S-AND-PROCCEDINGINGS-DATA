# 05 - Collector Proceedings

## What goes here

Proceedings issued by **District Collectors** that relate to PMEGP implementation, industrial estate development, or micro-enterprise promotion at the district level.

## Why Collector proceedings matter

District Collectors chair the **District Level Task Force** that reviews PMEGP applications above a certain threshold. Their proceedings document:
- District-level PMEGP monitoring committee decisions
- Land allotment for PMEGP-backed enterprises in industrial estates
- Inter-departmental coordination (between DIC, banks, revenue dept)
- Emergency or time-sensitive directives (e.g., deadline extensions)
- District-level priority sector lending reviews

## Primary sources

| Source | URL | Access Method |
|--------|-----|---------------|
| District Collector offices | Varies by district | Physical visit / RTI |
| District administration portals | Varies by district | Web |
| AP Revenue Department | `https://revenue.ap.gov.in` | Web |

## Collection challenges

- **No centralized portal** — each collectorate has different practices
- Proceedings are often **not published online** — RTI is the primary route
- Collector proceedings are broader than PMEGP — **filter carefully** for relevance

## Naming convention

```
PMEGP-AP-YYYY-NNN_districtcode_descriptive_slug.pdf
PMEGP-AP-YYYY-NNN_districtcode_descriptive_slug.metadata.json
```

## Notes

- Only include proceedings that **directly relate** to PMEGP or credit-linked subsidy schemes
- General district administration proceedings should NOT be included here
- Collector proceedings often reference DIC proceedings — link via `related_document_ids`
