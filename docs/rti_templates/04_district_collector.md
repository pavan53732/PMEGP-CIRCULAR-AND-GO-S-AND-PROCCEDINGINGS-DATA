# RTI Application — Priority 4: District Collectors (Visakhapatnam & Anakapalli)

**Target PIO**: District Collector's Public Information Officer, for both Visakhapatnam and Anakapalli districts. **File two separate applications** — one per district.

**Priority**: 4 — District-level DIC, DLCC, DLRC, Lead Bank, and Collector records.

**Estimated Fee**: ₹50 per application × 2 districts = ₹100 total (₹20 for BPL with certificate)

**Expected Response Time**: 30 days from filing

**Background**: District-level documents are the highest-volume tier and the hardest to acquire online. The District Collector chairs the District Level Monitoring Committee (DLMC), and the DIC / DLCC / DLRC / Lead Bank all report to or coordinate with the Collector. Per [`docs/DISTRICT_PMEGP_PROGRESS_FY2024_25.md`](../DISTRICT_PMEGP_PROGRESS_FY2024_25.md):
- **Visakhapatnam**: 26 target, 27 sanctioned, 14 disbursed (₹80.70 Lakh). Lead Bank: SBI.
- **Anakapalli**: 55 target, 159 sanctioned, 26 disbursed (₹100.96 Lakh, 65% gap). Lead Bank: Union Bank of India.

```
To: The Public Information Officer

    Visakhapatnam District

    Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh - 530001

Subject: Application under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005

Sir/Madam,

I, [APPLICANT_NAME], citizen of India, hereby request the following
information under the Right to Information Act, 2005, related to the
implementation of the Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme
(PMEGP) in Visakhapatnam District:

1. Certified copy of the District Industries Centre (DIC) Visakhapatnam
   annual target allocation proceeding for FY 2024-25, FY 2025-26, and
   FY 2026-27 (if issued), showing the KVIC / KVIB / DIC split of
   PMEGP physical and financial targets for the district.

2. Certified copies of the District Level Consultative Committee (DLCC)
   meeting minutes for Visakhapatnam District held during the period
   01 April 2024 to [DATE_OF_FILING], including:
   (a) Joint presentations by the DIC General Manager, KVIB District
       Officer, and KVIC representative
   (b) Bank-wise target vs. achievement sheets
   (c) Lead Bank (State Bank of India) participation and directives

3. Certified copies of the District Level Review Committee (DLRC)
   half-yearly credit performance review reports for Visakhapatnam
   District for FY 2024-25 and FY 2025-26, including:
   (a) Bank-wise PMEGP margin money claim and disbursement tracking
   (b) NPA / recovery analysis for PMEGP-assisted units

4. Certified copies of the Lead Bank (State Bank of India) directives
   and Annual Lead Bank Credit Plan for Visakhapatnam District for
   FY 2024-25, FY 2025-26, and FY 2026-27 (if issued), with specific
   reference to PMEGP sub-targets allocated to SBI branches.

5. Certified copies of the District Level Monitoring Committee (DLMC)
   review logs chaired by the District Collector, Visakhapatnam, that
   reference PMEGP, during the period 01 April 2024 to [DATE_OF_FILING].

6. Certified copies of all DIC Visakhapatnam sponsoring letters
   forwarding vetted PMEGP application profiles to financing banks,
   during the period 01 April 2024 to [DATE_OF_FILING], in aggregated
   form (with beneficiary PII redacted per Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI
   Act).

7. A list of all DIC / DLCC / DLRC / Lead Bank / Collector proceedings,
   circulars, and minutes issued in Visakhapatnam District on PMEGP
   during the period 01 April 2024 to [DATE_OF_FILING], with their
   reference numbers, dates, and subject lines.

I request that the information be provided in the form of certified
photocopies of the original documents. I am enclosing the prescribed
RTI fee of ₹50 (Rupees Fifty Only) via [PAYMENT_MODE].

If the requested information is exempt under Section 8 or 9 of the RTI
Act, please provide the specific exemption clause and the reasons for
invoking it, along with the details of the First Appellate Authority.

[APPLICANT_NAME]
[APPLICANT_ADDRESS]
[APPLICANT_PHONE]
[APPLICANT_EMAIL]
[DATE_OF_FILING]

Place: [CITY]
```

## Filing Instructions

1. **Online filing** at <https://rtionline.gov.in>:
   - Select "Andhra Pradesh" as the State
   - Search for "District Collector, Visakhapatnam" (and "Anakapalli" separately)
   - File two separate applications

2. **Physical filing**:
   - Print both applications
   - Attach a ₹50 IPO per application (₹100 total)
   - Send via Speed Post to:
     ```
     The Public Information Officer
     Office of the District Collector
     Visakhapatnam District
     Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh - 530001
     ```
     and:
     ```
     The Public Information Officer
     ```

## Important Notes

- **PII Redaction**: Item 6 explicitly requests aggregated / redacted copies. If the PIO provides unredacted beneficiary lists, you MUST redact Aadhaar / bank account / phone numbers before committing to the archive per [`docs/collection_policy.md`](../collection_policy.md) §3.
- **First Appeal**: District Collector offices often delay or reject RTI applications. Be prepared to file first appeals within 30 days of the response deadline.
- **Cross-Reference**: Once received, cross-reference these district-level documents against the [`docs/DISTRICT_PMEGP_PROGRESS_FY2024_25.md`](../DISTRICT_PMEGP_PROGRESS_FY2024_25.md) table to verify that the figures match the state-level reporting.
