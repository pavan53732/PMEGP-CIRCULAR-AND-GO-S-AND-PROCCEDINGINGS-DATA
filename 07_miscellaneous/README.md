# 07 - Miscellaneous

## What goes here

Documents from other authorities that are relevant to PMEGP but don't fit into the six specific categories above.

## Included document types

### MoMSME Notifications
- Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises policy circulars
- National-level PMEGP guideline revisions
- Annual reports and performance data

### RBI Circulars
- Priority sector lending guidelines affecting PMEGP
- MSME loan classification and restructuring circulars
- Interest subvention scheme notifications

### NABARD Publications
- Refinance scheme guidelines for PMEGP
- State-level MSME credit flow analysis
- Rural entrepreneurship development reports

### Press Information Bureau (PIB) Releases
- Government press releases on PMEGP launches, milestones
- Scheme awareness campaign materials

### Court Orders
- High Court or Supreme Court orders affecting PMEGP implementation
- Writ petitions related to subsidy disbursement delays
- PIL outcomes on scheme transparency

### State Assembly Questions & Answers
- Unstarred questions on PMEGP in AP Legislative Assembly
- Ministerial replies with scheme data

## Naming convention

```
PMEGP-AP-YYYY-NNN_source_descriptive_slug.pdf
PMEGP-AP-YYYY-NNN_source_descriptive_slug.metadata.json
```

## Notes
- Only include documents that **directly reference PMEGP** or credit-linked subsidy schemes for micro-enterprises
- General MSME policy documents without specific PMEGP content should be excluded
- Court orders can be lengthy — extract only the relevant pages/sections if possible
