# 02 - KVIC Andhra Pradesh Letters

## What goes here

Official letters, directives, and communications from **KVIC (Khadi and Village Industries Commission)** regional and district offices operating in Andhra Pradesh.

## Scope

- Target allocation letters to DICs
- Application processing guidelines and clarifications
- Subsidy disbursement instructions
- Training program directives
- Monthly/quarterly review meeting communications
- Audit and compliance-related letters

## KVIC offices relevant to AP

| Office | Jurisdiction | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| KVIC Regional Office, Hyderabad | Telangana & AP (shared pre-bifurcation) | Many legacy letters still relevant |
| KVIC State Office, Andhra Pradesh | Andhra Pradesh | Post-bifurcation dedicated office |
| KVIC Zonal Office | Southern Zone | Policy-level directives affecting AP |

## Primary sources

| Source | URL | Access Method |
|--------|-----|---------------|
| KVIC Official Portal | `https://www.kvic.gov.in` | Limited public access |
| RTI to KVIC | Via `rtionline.gov.in` | Formal request required |
| KVIC Regional Office | Physical visit | By appointment |
| DIC forwarded copies | Via DIC offices | Often available at DICs |

## Challenges

- KVIC letters are **not systematically published online**
- Many are available only as physical copies at DICs or KVIC offices
- RTI requests may be the most reliable collection method
- Some letters are in Hindi — include transliteration in keywords
