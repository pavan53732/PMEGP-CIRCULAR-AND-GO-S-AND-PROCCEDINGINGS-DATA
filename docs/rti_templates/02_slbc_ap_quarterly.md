# RTI Application — Priority 2: SLBC AP Convenor Bank

**Target PIO**: Public Information Officer, SLBC AP Convenor Bank Office (typically the Lead Bank of the state, which rotates — currently believed to be Andhra Bank / Union Bank of India; verify on <https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in>).

**Priority**: 2 — Quarterly SLBC records (agendas, minutes, district reviews).

**Estimated Fee**: ₹50 (general) / ₹10 (BPL with certificate)

**Expected Response Time**: 30 days from filing

**Background**: The SLBC AP portal (`slbcap.nic.in`) is DNS-unresolvable and the Wayback Machine snapshot is only a splash page. RTI is the only viable acquisition route.

## Filing Instructions

1. **Identify the Convenor Bank** — check the latest RBI SLBC list at
   <https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewSLBC.aspx>. As of the last
   review, the SLBC AP Convenor rotates among major public sector banks.

2. **Online filing** at <https://rtionline.gov.in>:
   - Select "Andhra Pradesh" as the State
   - Search for "SLBC" or "State Level Bankers Committee"
   - If not listed, file against the Convenor Bank directly (e.g., "Union Bank of India" or "State Bank of India")

3. **Physical filing**:
   - Print the application
   - Attach a ₹50 IPO marked to the Convenor Bank
   - Send via Speed Post to the SLBC Convenor Bank office
