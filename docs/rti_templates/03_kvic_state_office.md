# RTI Application — Priority 3: KVIC State Director, Andhra Pradesh

**Target PIO**: Public Information Officer, KVIC State Office (Andhra Pradesh), Vijayawada / Visakhapatnam.

**Priority**: 3 — KVIC AP State Office review letters, action plans, EDP calendar.

**Estimated Fee**: ₹50 (general) / ₹10 (BPL with certificate)

**Expected Response Time**: 30 days from filing

**Background**: KVIC is the national nodal agency for PMEGP. The KVIC State Office in AP (located in Vijayawada/Visakhapatnam) issues review letters to financing banks regarding delayed margin money releases, allocates the state's KVIC share (30%) of PMEGP targets, and conducts EDP training for PMEGP candidates routed through the KVIC channel.

---

## Filing Instructions

1. **Identify the KVIC State Office address** — check
   <https://kvic.org.in> → "State Offices" section, or call the KVIC
   HQ at +91-22-26713686.

2. **Online filing** at <https://rtionline.gov.in>:
   - Select "Central Government" (KVIC is a Central statutory body)
   - Search for "Khadi and Village Industries Commission"
   - Select "State Office, Andhra Pradesh" as the section

3. **Physical filing**:
   - Print the application
   - Attach a ₹50 IPO marked to "Khadi and Village Industries Commission"
   - Send via Speed Post to the KVIC State Office, AP
