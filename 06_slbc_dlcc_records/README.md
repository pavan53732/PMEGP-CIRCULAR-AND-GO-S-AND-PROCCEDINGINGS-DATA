# 06 - SLBC/DLCC Records

## What goes here

Records from the **State Level Bankers' Committee (SLBC)** and **District Level Consultative Committee (DLCC)** that relate to PMEGP credit flow, subsidy disbursement, and banking guidelines.

## Why these records matter

PMEGP is a **credit-linked subsidy scheme** — banks are the primary channel for fund disbursement. SLBC/DLCC records contain:
- Annual/monthly credit flow targets for PMEGP under priority sector lending
- Bank-wise PMEGP loan disbursement data
- Subsidy release coordination instructions (between banks, KVIC, and DICs)
- Interest rate guidelines and refinance facilities
- Default and NPA data related to PMEGP accounts
- District-level banking coordination meeting minutes

## Key institutions

| Institution | Role |
|-------------|------|
| SLBC Andhra Pradesh | State-level coordination of all banks on government schemes |
| Lead Bank (SBI for AP) | Convenor of SLBC/DLCC meetings, primary data compiler |
| NABARD | Refinancing agency, monitors credit flow to MSE sector |
| RBI | Regulatory guidelines on priority sector lending including PMEGP |
| District Lead Banks | Convenors of DLCC meetings at the district level |

## Notes

- SLBC/DLCC records may be in **Excel or PDF** format — standardize to PDF for the archive
- Credit flow data is often **aggregate** (not individual applicant data) — less PII concern
- NABARD's annual reports on PMEGP are a goldmine for state-level statistics
- Some DLCC minutes may be confidential — verify before uploading
