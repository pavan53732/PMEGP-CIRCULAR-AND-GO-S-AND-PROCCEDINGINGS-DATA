# 03 - Commissioner of Industries Proceedings

## What goes here

Proceedings issued by the **Commissioner/Director of Industries, Government of Andhra Pradesh**. These are operational directives that translate state-level Government Orders into actionable instructions for District Industries Centres (DICs) and field offices.

## Why this category matters

The Commissioner of Industries is the **key middle layer** between state policy (GOs) and district-level implementation (DICs). Proceedings from this office often contain:
- Target allocations for each district under PMEGP
- Application processing timelines and deadlines
- Clarifications on eligibility criteria
- Instructions for subsidy claim processing
- Review meeting outcomes and follow-up actions

## Proceeding number format

Commissioner proceedings typically follow: `Rc.No. XXX / Commissioner of Industries / Date`

## Notes

- Proceedings are often issued in English with Telugu translations
- Some proceedings reference earlier proceedings — always check `related_document_ids`
- Proceedings may contain annexures (appendices) as separate pages — keep as single PDF if possible
