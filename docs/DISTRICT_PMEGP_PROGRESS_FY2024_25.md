# PMEGP District-wise Progress Reference (FY 2024-25)

This document captures the **complete district-wise PMEGP progress table** for Andhra Pradesh for FY 2024-25, extracted from the AP Socio-Economic Survey 2024-25 (`AP-GOV-2025-0001.pdf`, Annexure 6.4, page 102).

This is the **definitive baseline** for targeting district-level document acquisition. It tells us exactly which districts have the most active PMEGP pipelines and therefore which District Industries Centres (DICs), Lead Banks, and District Level Consultative Committees (DLCCs) should be producing the highest volume of PMEGP-related proceedings.

## 2. State-Level PMEGP Scheme Summary (from the same survey)

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Generate employment in rural and urban areas through self-employment ventures |
| **Implementing Agencies** | KVIC, KVIBs, State Governments, and District Industries Centres (DICs) |
| **Eligibility** | Self Help Groups (incl. BPL), General and Special Categories (SC/ST/OBC/Minorities/Women, Ex-servicemen, Physically handicapped, NER Hill and Border area) |
| **Beneficiary Contribution** | 10% of project cost (General), 5% (Special) |
| **Margin Money Subsidy Rate (Urban)** | 15% (General), 25% (Special) |
| **Margin Money Subsidy Rate (Rural)** | 25% (General), 35% (Special) |
| **Assistance Type** | One-time assistance |
| **Repayment Schedule** | 3 to 7 years |

## 4. Tripartite Implementation Reality

Per the official PMEGP guidelines (see `IN-MSME-2022-0001` and `IN-KVIC-2022-0001`), PMEGP is implemented through a **strict tripartite division** at both the state and district levels:

1. **KVIC** (Khadi and Village Industries Commission) — Central statutory body, state-level directorates
2. **KVIB** (State Khadi and Village Industries Boards) — State-level statutory bodies
3. **DICs** (District Industries Centres) — State government directorates under the Commissioner of Industries

Target allocations are distributed across these three channels. The historical ratio is approximately:

- **KVIC: 30%** of total targets
- **KVIB: 30%** of total targets
- **DIC: 40%** of total targets

(Or in some fiscal years, a 60:40 split between KVIC/KVIB combined and DICs, depending on rural/urban target composition.)

This means each district's target (e.g. Visakhapatnam: 26 projects, Anakapalli: 55 projects) is further subdivided three ways, and each channel issues its own proceedings and review letters.

## 6. What This Means for Document Acquisition

For each priority district, we need to acquire the following document types from the tripartite channels:

### A. DIC Proceedings (District Industries Centres)
- Annual target allocation proceedings (showing the KVIC/KVIB/DIC split)
- Quarterly monitoring instructions from the Commissioner of Industries
- Physical verification reports (now geo-tagged per `IN-KVIC-2022-0002`)
- Sponsoring letters forwarding vetted applications to financing banks

### B. DLCC Minutes (District Level Consultative Committee)
- Quarterly meeting minutes with bank-wise target vs. achievement sheets
- Joint presentations by DIC GM, KVIB District Officer, and KVIC representative
- Lead Bank Manager participation (SBI for Visakhapatnam, Union Bank for Anakapalli)

### C. DLRC Reports (District Level Review Committee)
- Half-yearly credit performance reviews
- Bank-wise PMEGP margin money claim and disbursement tracking
- NPA/recovery analysis for PMEGP-assisted units

### D. Lead Bank Directives
- Annual Lead Bank credit plans (with PMEGP-specific sub-targets)
- Handholding and RSETI setup guidelines
- Bank-wise target allocation circulars

### E. Collector Proceedings (District Collector)
- District Level Monitoring Committee (DLMC) review logs
- Collector-level PMEGP review meetings (typically quarterly)

## 7. Key Findings & Anomalies

### Visakhapatnam Underperformance
Visakhapatnam has the **lowest project target** of all 26 districts (only 26 projects vs. the state average of ~60). This is unusual for a district with Visakhapatnam's industrial base. Possible explanations to investigate:
- Most PMEGP activity may have shifted to the newly carved Anakapalli district (created in 2022)
- Visakhapatnam may be classified as "urban" for PMEGP purposes, attracting lower subsidy rates
- Industrial concentration in larger MSMEs (above PMEGP's micro-enterprise threshold)

### Anakapalli High Claim-to-Disbursement Gap
Anakapalli sanctioned 159 projects with margin money claims of ₹289.81 Lakh, but only ₹100.96 Lakh (35%) was actually disbursed. This 65% gap suggests:
- Bank-level delays in margin money adjustment
- Potential DLCC/DLRC discussion topics worth acquiring minutes for
- KVIC state office review letters to banks regarding delayed releases (Tier 4 target)

### State-Level Aggregates
- **Total target**: 1,553 projects / ₹4,400.40 Lakh MM
- **Total sanctioned**: 3,054 projects / ₹12,068 Lakh MM (197% of target by project count)
- **Total claimed**: 3,837 projects / ₹13,731 Lakh MM
- **Total disbursed**: 1,443 projects / ₹7,198.20 Lakh MM (only 47% of sanctioned MM disbursed)

The state-wide disbursement gap (53%) is a major policy issue that should appear in SLBC AP quarterly minutes and Commissioner of Industries review proceedings.
