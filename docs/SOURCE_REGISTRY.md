# PMEGP Official Document Source Registry

This document registers and indexes every official source, portal, and section where PMEGP-related circulars, GOs, proceedings, and guidelines are published. This serves as the definitive reference guide for contributors collecting files.

---

## 1. National Level & Central Government Sources

| Authority | Official Website | Targeted Section | File Format | Priority | Notes / Access Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ministry of MSME** | [msme.gov.in](https://msme.gov.in) | Scheme Guidelines / Documents | PDF | **High** | Primary source for central policy notifications and Finance Commission revisions. |
| **KVIC Head Office** | [kvic.org.in](https://kvic.org.in) | Circulars / Portal Updates | PDF | **High** | Look for the "PMEGP" specific sub-tab under the main Circulars section. |
| **KVIC PMEGP Portal** | [kviconline.gov.in](https://www.kviconline.gov.in) | PMEGP e-Portal / Notifications | PDF | **High** | Houses the actual online application guidelines and direct notification PDFs. |
| **Reserve Bank of India** | [rbi.org.in](https://rbi.org.in) | Notifications / Master Circulars | PDF | **Medium** | Look for credit-linked subsidy guidelines under Rural Planning & Credit Department (RPCD). |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Search Strategy for New Contributors

To maximize success when crawling or manually searching these sites, use the following standardized search terms in local search boxes and search engines:

1. `"PMEGP"`
2. `"Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme"`
3. `"Margin Money Subsidy"`
4. `"District Level Consultative Committee"` (or `"DLCC"`)
5. `"State Level Bankers' Committee"` (or `"SLBC"`)
